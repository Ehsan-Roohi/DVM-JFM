Main text, numerical evidence section: Main_DSMC_high_moments
Mach-3 hard-sphere DSMC high-order moments at PPC=960, 1920 and 3840. Curves are means over eight independent seeds; shaded bands are pointwise 95% Student-t intervals (7 degrees of freedom). Each time-averaged profile is aligned at its n=2 crossing using linear interpolation, without smoothing. The coordinate is scaled by the upstream mean free path (lambda_1=1 in these DSMC units). Bands quantify between-seed variability, not systematic error or simultaneous coverage. These data use the original collision sampler; the identified pair-selection bias remains under separate assessment. Small inter-PPC differences do not establish sampler correctness or remove temporal shock wandering.

Mach-5 BGK DVM numerical sensitivity. Left: 100 ||f_base-f_test||_2/||f_test||_2 for each displayed moment, evaluated on 601 equally spaced points in |(x-x_s)/lambda_1|<=15 after independent density-midpoint alignment. Right: the same relative norm between the final two saved profiles of each case. Saved-step pairs are baseline 50000-52000, velocity extent 62000-62400, velocity refinement 48000-50000, spatial refinement 76000-78000, half time step 102000-104000 and spatial extent 50000-52000. These unequal intervals measure late change, not comparable time derivatives or a new stopping criterion. The velocity-grid case (*) uses the provisional 50000-step profile, before its prescribed 52000-step end, so that comparison also contains a physical-time mismatch. Baseline velocity quadrature is 141x27x27; enlarged velocity domain uses 169x33x33 and refined velocity grid 211x40x40. Baseline v_max=22, enlarged longitudinal v_max=26.4 and transverse bound=27.076923. Spatial refinement is 2200 to 3299 cells; spatial half-domain is 80 to 120 upstream mean free paths (3300 cells). Spatial refinement also changes the CFL-controlled time step. Two levels demonstrate sensitivity, not asymptotic order or exact-solution error. Delta_DVM is defined relative to the discrete Maxwellian.

Spatial distribution of moment differences for the same aligned Mach-5 DVM comparisons as A1. The ordinate is 100(f_test-f_base)/max|f_base|, with one peak normalization per field over the plotted interval, not a pointwise relative error near zero crossings. Main DSMC results, or Appendix B with an explicit main-text numerical summary: A3_DSMC_collision_source
Particle-number sensitivity of the directly logged Mach-3 scalar collision source. (a) Eight-seed mean Q_Delta^collision and pointwise 95% Student-t intervals for PPC=960, 1920 and 3840. (b) Relative L2 differences between adjacent PPC ensemble means, normalized by the higher-PPC mean on the common aligned interval. (c) Mean accumulated accepted-collision counts per cell, interpolated to the same aligned coordinate; these counts are not independent sample counts or effective sample sizes. All cases use 400 cells, dt=0.005, 180000 steps, and sampling after 70000 steps at every tenth step. No spatial smoothing is used in these plots. The final adjacent source difference is 0.6683%. This audit holds the sampling schedule fixed and does not establish sampling-window independence or confirm the previously reported source/gradient correlation of 0.987. Original collision-sampler systematic bias remains unresolved; these curves cannot by themselves support a bias-free Boltzmann validation claim.

Main text, sparse-recovery results: A4_sparse_core_gap
Controlled stress test of sparse scalar-channel interpolation on the completed Mach-5 baseline. The active zone is |Delta|>=0.02 max|Delta| after subtraction of the mean over the outermost 8% at each end, following the existing interpolation code. Active-uniform probes cover the active interval padded by 25% on each side; a second layout leaves a gap of width 25% of the active interval centered at the largest |Delta|. Each of these layouts has 24 probes plus two boundary anchors (26 total), with respectively 16 and 14 probes inside the active interval. The existing full-domain-uniform layout has 24 total probes including its endpoints, seven inside the active interval. Thus the full-domain comparison is not an exactly equal-count experiment; the active-uniform versus core-gap comparison is. Gaussian RBF hyperparameters are selected solely from sparse Delta values by leave-one-out validation over 28/32/48 centers, widths 0.5/0.6/0.8/1/1.2/1.5 times center spacing, and ridge values 1e-10/1e-8/1e-6/1e-4/1e-2. No noise is added. For this diagnostic S is supplied exactly as R+Delta/3, isolating interpolation error rather than testing budget-derived S. Active-zone selection uses the reference field, so this is an oracle-controlled test, not blind sensor placement. Active-zone R errors are 3.107%, 33.014% and 17.385%, respectively. Successful leave-one-out fits can fail in unobserved gaps; sensor coverage must accompany any sparse-recovery claim. The result is one case/layout realization, not a universal failure threshold.
